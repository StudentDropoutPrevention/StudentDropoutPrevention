Dropout Prevention Program

Shwapnojatra Foundation works to reduce school dropout through educational support, awareness programs, family engagement and community participation. Our goal is to create a society where every child has the opportunity to complete their education.

Our Mission

To ensure that no child is left behind due to poverty, social barriers or lack of educational opportunities. We aim to support vulnerable children and encourage them to continue their education.

Our Key Activities

📚 Educational Support

Providing educational materials and learning support to disadvantaged students.

🎓 Scholarship Support

Supporting talented and financially disadvantaged students through educational assistance.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family Awareness

Encouraging parents to understand the importance of education and regular school attendance.

🏫 Community Engagement

Working with teachers, parents and communities to prevent school dropout.

👧 Girls' Education

Promoting equal educational opportunities for girls and boys.

🤝 Student Follow-up

Identifying at-risk students and encouraging them to continue their education.

Our Vision

A dropout-free society where every child has access to inclusive, equitable and quality education.
