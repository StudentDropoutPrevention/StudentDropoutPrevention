Who We Are

Shwapnojatra Foundation is a social development initiative committed to improving the quality of life of disadvantaged and vulnerable communities. Our organization aims to promote education, social awareness, healthcare, environmental protection, youth development and community empowerment. We believe that every individual deserves equal opportunities to live with dignity and build a better future. Through community participation and collective efforts, we aim to contribute to sustainable social development.

Our Vision, Mission & Goals

🌍 Our Vision

To build an educated, healthy, inclusive and sustainable society where every person has equal opportunities and dignity.

🎯 Our Mission

To empower disadvantaged communities through education, healthcare, awareness, skills development and sustainable social initiatives.

🤝 Our Goals

To reduce poverty, improve educational opportunities, promote social equality and encourage community participation in development activities.

Our Objectives

  • Promote quality education for disadvantaged children.
  • Prevent school dropout and encourage school attendance.
  • Provide educational support and scholarship opportunities.
  • Promote healthcare awareness and community health activities.
  • Encourage environmental protection and tree plantation.
  • Develop youth leadership and volunteer activities.
  • Promote women's empowerment and social equality.
  • Support poverty reduction and sustainable livelihood initiatives.

Our Core Values

Integrity

Equality

We promote equal opportunities for all members of society.

Humanity

We work with compassion, respect and dignity for everyone.

Commitment

We are committed to positive and sustainable social change.
